## Revised website copy

I turn business bottlenecks into working automation.

I’m Yassir. I find where work gets stuck, then build software that removes the repeated effort—from scattered supplier data to disconnected systems.

I work on the parts of a business that keep slowing people down.

That might be supplier data that needs cleaning every week, details copied out of documents, or the same update entered into several tools. I start by understanding the work and why it gets stuck, then build around the way the team needs it to run.

My background in electrical engineering and process improvement shapes how I build software: trace the cause, make the result easy to check, and account for the exceptions people will still need to handle.
